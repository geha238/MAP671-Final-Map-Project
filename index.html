Museums in Manhattan, New York

Author: Grace Haines
Software: QGIS 3.44.15
Projection: EPSG: 3857

This map shows all of the museums in Manhattan. I was inspired to make this map because of a recent trip I took to New York to visit museums, and I wanted to see how many museums were actually in New York, specifically Manhattan.
